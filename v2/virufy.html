01 — The brief

The brief

Virufy had already launched their application in Japan. They wanted to work with a team in Dubai to launch it in the UAE, with a focus on adapting the application to the cultural differences of the Middle East.

Why this problem matters

Easier access to health screening can help many people who may not be physically able to go to clinics or hospitals.

02 — The process

From Japan to the UAE

01

How Virufy works

Virufy’s app uses AI to analyse cough and breathing sounds with deep-learning algorithms and audio signal processing. The algorithm examines elements of the cough such as duration, frequency and pitch, then compares the recorded patterns against a database of known indicators to identify signals associated with respiratory conditions.

02

My part

As an intern, I was on the team that designed and deployed the application for the UAE. Taking inspiration from the Japanese team’s work, we developed the designs in Figma and coded them in React. The deployment let people try a demo straight from the Virufy website.
